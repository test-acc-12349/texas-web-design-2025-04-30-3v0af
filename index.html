About Texas Web Design

Founded in 2015 by a team of passionate web developers and designers, Texas Web Design has grown from a small local agency to one of the state's leading digital service providers. Our journey began with a simple mission: to provide Texas businesses with world-class websites that drive real results.

Our core values of innovation, integrity, and client success guide everything we do. We believe in building long-term partnerships with our clients, delivering solutions that not only meet their immediate needs but support their long-term growth objectives. Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in your web design packages?

Our comprehensive packages include custom design, responsive development, SEO optimization, SSL certification, hosting, and ongoing support. Each website is built with conversion optimization in mind.

How long does the design process take?

Most projects are completed within 3-6 weeks, depending on complexity. We follow a structured timeline: discovery (1 week), design (1-2 weeks), development (1-2 weeks), and testing/launch (1 week).

What makes your hosting service different?

Our enterprise-grade hosting includes daily backups, 99.9% uptime guarantee, CDN integration, and advanced security measures. We handle all technical aspects so you can focus on your business.

Do you offer post-launch support?

Yes, we provide comprehensive post-launch support including maintenance, updates, security monitoring, and content updates. Our support team is available 24/7 for urgent issues.

What payment options do you offer?

We offer flexible payment plans including monthly installments, milestone-based payments, and pay-in-full options. All major credit cards and bank transfers are accepted.
